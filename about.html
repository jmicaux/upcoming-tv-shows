About Upcoming TV Shows

A free, ad-free calendar of new and returning TV series: premiere dates month by month, across US, UK, Australian, Nordic, French and streaming channels.

What it does

  • Lists upcoming episodes and premieres, month by month, for the channels you follow.
  • Keeps a watchlist of your favourite shows with their next episode.
  • Links to where each show can be watched.
  • Works without an account; an optional account keeps your settings on every device.

Where the data comes from

Schedules come from TVmaze (US, UK, Australian, Nordic and streaming channels) and TMDB (French channels). They are refreshed every night and shown as they are published by these sources, which can change dates at short notice. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Terms of use

The service is provided free of charge and as is, without any guarantee of accuracy or availability. Show titles, images and descriptions belong to their respective owners and come from the sources above.

Accessibility

The site aims at WCAG 2.2 level AA: keyboard and screen-reader friendly, sufficient contrast, reduced motion respected. Please report any barrier. See the accessibility statement.

Support

Upcoming TV Shows is free, with no ads and no tracking cookies. If you find it useful, you can buy me a coffee.

Contact and feedback

Questions, bugs or suggestions: open an issue on GitHub. The source code is public in the same repository.
