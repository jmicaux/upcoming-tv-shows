Accessibility statement

The publisher of Upcoming TV Shows is committed to making the site accessible, in line with the French accessibility standard RGAA 4.1.2 (based on WCAG 2.1 level AA). This statement applies to jmicaux.github.io/upcoming-tv-shows.

Compliance status

The site is non-compliant with RGAA 4.1.2: no full audit of its 106 criteria has been carried out yet. This is the status the RGAA requires when no audit exists; it does not mean the site is inaccessible (see the measures below).

Measures already taken

  • Fully keyboard operable, visible focus, a “Skip to shows” link.
  • Heading structure (one main heading per view), declared language, landmarks (header, navigation, main content, footer).
  • Contrast checked at level AA (text ≥ 4.5:1, interface components ≥ 3:1), including the red header.
  • Explicit names for buttons and links (show, channel, opening in a new tab); labelled form fields.
  • Dialogs keep focus inside and return it on close; they close with Escape or the Back button.
  • Touch targets of at least 44 px on mobile; no action requires a complex gesture.
  • The “reduce motion” setting is respected; no text smaller than 12 px.

Known non-accessible content

None known at the date of this statement.

Content not covered

  • The Google and GitHub sign-in pages, and the third-party sites behind “Watch on…” links.
  • Show texts and images supplied by TVmaze and TMDB.

How this statement was prepared

This statement was prepared on November 11, 2026, after an AI-assisted RGAA self-assessment of the code (not a conformity audit). Of the 106 criteria, 43 do not apply; of the 63 that apply, 32 are compliant, none is non-compliant, and 31 still need checking by a person (screen reader and human judgement). Keyboard use, zoom and reflow, text spacing, contrast, page titles, reading without styles and screen orientation were checked by hand. The 16 non-conformities found during the assessment have been fixed.

  • Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WAI-ARIA.
  • Testing tools: WAVE (0 errors), ARC Toolkit (0 errors), Lighthouse (accessibility 96–100), html-validate.
  • Test environment: Firefox on desktop, with mobile emulation.
  • Pages checked: home, channel pages, Watchlist, search, page not found, About, Privacy.

Feedback and contact

If you cannot access some content or a feature, please report it by opening an issue on GitHub: we will offer an accessible alternative and fix the problem.

Seeking redress

If you reported an accessibility problem and did not receive a satisfactory answer, you can contact the French Défenseur des droits (Defender of Rights):

  • online: Défenseur des droits contact form;
