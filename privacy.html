Privacy

Upcoming TV Shows works without an account: your channels, watchlist and settings stay in your browser. This page describes what changes if you choose to sign in.

Without an account

Nothing about you is sent to us. Preferences are kept in your browser's local storage. The app fetches public schedule data from TVMaze and TMDB, and page views are counted with Umami, a cookieless analytics service that does not identify you.

With an account — what is stored

  • Your email address (from Google or GitHub, or the one you type in), used only to recognise you when you sign in.
  • Your preferences: followed channels, watchlist, watch redirects and theme, as one small record.

Your TMDB API key is never synced: it stays on the device where you entered it.

Why

So that you find the same channels, watchlist and settings on every device you sign in from. That is the only purpose.

Where and for how long

Accounts and preferences are stored by Supabase in a European Union data centre. Only you can read or change your record. It is kept until you delete your account.

Sign-in providers

"Continue with Google" asks Google for your email address and name only. Google's own privacy policy applies to that step.

"Continue with GitHub" asks GitHub for your email address and public profile (username, name). GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that step.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account in the app. This removes your email address and your preferences immediately. For any other request, open an issue on GitHub.

Third parties

  • TVMaze and TMDB — schedule data (no account data is sent to them).
  • Umami — cookieless page-view counts.
  • Supabase — account and preferences storage (only if you sign in).
  • Google — sign-in (only if you choose it).
  • GitHub — sign-in (only if you choose it).
